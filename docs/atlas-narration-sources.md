# Atlas narration — expanded photographic details

Seven new narrated details cover every image added to the atlas. They complement the four Swiss and Rotorua details already in the documentary. The scripts use short, contemporary editorial narration and describe visible features or documented context. They do not supply missing personal identities, exposure dates or exact camera locations.

## Image and source review

All six local JPEGs were inspected before selecting the hotspot coordinates. For the portrait crops, the hotspot sits near the eyes or centre of the face and uses a moderate 1.35 zoom. The two-image Peru plate has separate points for the people in the upper image and the fitted stonework in the lower image.

Primary source checked: [Weston A. Price, Nutrition and Physical Degeneration](https://gutenberg.net.au/ebooks02/0200251h.html).

The scripts distinguish published geographic context from visible-image observations. They do not repeat the book's racial typologies or treat historical medical conclusions as contemporary evidence. The narration does not infer the subject's feelings or personal circumstances from a facial expression.

## Audio assets

New files are local MP3s under public/narration, named detail-<detail-id>.mp3. The matching transcript is in lib/experience/detail-script.json; sentence cues and duration are in detail-manifest.json. Existing scripts and clips were retained. All clips use the existing synthetic English demo narrator, en-GB-RyanNeural at -12%, through scripts/generate-narration.py. This is not Price's voice or archival sound.
