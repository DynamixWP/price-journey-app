## Primary source

[Weston A. Price, Nutrition and Physical Degeneration](https://gutenberg.net.au/ebooks02/0200251h.html):

- Chapter 13, Figure 82 and its preceding paragraph identify Sacsayhuamán in the upper photograph. The complete two-photo plate is retained. The lower image is not assigned the upper image’s exact location.
- Chapter 8, Figure 35 explicitly identifies Rarotonga. The two supplied original crops correspond to the two upper portraits in that figure.
- Chapter 12, Figure 69 provides the New Zealand context. The chapter states that the field investigations were limited to the North Island; these portraits are therefore kept separate from the Rotorua clinic marker. Personal names and individual settlements are unconfirmed.
- Chapter 7, the paragraph immediately before Figure 28 connects its subjects with New Caledonia. The map uses a regional reference.

## Geographic precision and interpretation

Map centers are approximate navigation references for a city area, island or region, not measured camera locations. Every card states this precision. No exact exposure dates or personal names were invented. The text describes the images and their historical source without adopting the book’s racial classifications or clinical conclusions as contemporary findings.

The promising supplied boat image NPD 300_Fig_173.tiff and the additional digitized negatives were not added to the map: the available files did not establish a reliable location. They remain available for later caption review.
